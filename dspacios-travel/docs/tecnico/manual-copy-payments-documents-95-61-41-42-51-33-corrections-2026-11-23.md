## 6. Remaining gaps / limits

- **Minimum sources:** the plan function revalidates payments, price, collection total, departure date and the id of the latest agreement under the lock; changes to `config_cobros` or `contrato_condiciones` inside that millisecond window are not detected (the plan is a suggested calendar; the minimum to CONFIRM is always decided by the database).
- **Quotation persistence** is in the quotation payload (and shown on the detail); `cotizacion_servicios` columns were not added because six RPC definitions would have to be replaced. The dynamic quotation → contract conversion does not copy category/regime/accommodation into a contract hotel row (dynamic contracts have none); the manual CONTRACT form already stores them.
- **Authoritative restriction** applies to hotels chosen by id; a hotel typed as free text is, by definition, not a catalog selection and is not checked. The contract does not persist the catalog link (only the copy).
- **Comparative → contract conversion** of the chosen option was not completed through the screen in this pass (the conversion form needs titular data); the chosen option's quotation carries its own tariff (verified in the database and on screen) and the conversion reads that same payload.
- `gerencia` cross-agency reach and the venta «Contrato no encontrado» on the plan action are pre-existing, unchanged, and recorded above.
- Hosted cost (Supabase/Vercel) is not measured; the advisory lock is per contract and short.
