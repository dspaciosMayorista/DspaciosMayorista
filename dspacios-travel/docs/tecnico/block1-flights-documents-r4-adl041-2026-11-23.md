## 2. Design decisions the owner may want to confirm (not invented silently)

1. **Service identity = shared saved PNR; key = anchor leg id.** It is the only saved datum that ties legs to a reservation. The PNR itself cannot be the key (it is usually saved *after* generation: «generate without PNR, save PNR, regenerate» must keep the link). The anchor id survives the flight editor (it preserves leg ids). If the superadmin contract-content editor re-creates the legs (new ids) and the contract has exactly one service and one air voucher, the voucher is re-keyed and keeps its link (verified, driver D1); with several vouchers nothing is guessed — an unknown key creates a new voucher and the old one stays (nothing is deleted; delete it by hand).
2. **Legs without a PNR next to legs with PNRs** are not assigned by guesswork: they get their own voucher and the staff are warned. Alternative would be to block, but the policy says absence of PNR must never block generation.
3. **PNR shown only with 100 % payment, also to internal preview.** The owner text says «paid voucher»; internal staff still see the voucher before payment, without the PNR and with a non-printing note.
4. Two PNRs = two services is a rule over saved data; if the business later wants «one reservation, several vouchers» or the reverse, that needs a saved service identity on the legs (new column), which does not exist today.
