## 1. Approved business rules

1. Explicit opt-in mode **«follow the winning base each night»**. A promotion spanning successive base seasons applies its percentage to the base that wins each night.
2. Priority resolution, genuine same-night ambiguity rejection, missing-rate safeguards and protection against double discounts are preserved.
3. Adults, Niño 1 and Niño 2 receive the percentage; **infants keep their base tariff** (in the new mode only).
4. Existing stored-row and linked-base promotions are unchanged unless explicitly edited into the new mode; their infant behavior (linked promotions still discount infants) is **not** silently corrected; historical documents are not repriced.
5. Optional **one free child per room with at least two adults**: per-room eligibility (no pooling), exactly one free child per room, promotion age range optional (else the hotel's age rules; no invented default age),
   applied together with the percentage, additional eligible children pay the promotional child tariff, infants unchanged, **only the promoted hotel's accommodation** (never other hotels, flights, assistance, transfers, tours, services), respecting the promotion's dates.

**Accommodation / meal-plan / additional-charge trace (decides how the free child is priced).** The sale price of a reservation is `Σ rooms × pax_tarifa × PVP(acomodación) + minors × PVP(class) + services + pet + rounding line`
(`lib/reservar/computo.ts`); the PVP of an accommodation is composed from the hotel net with markup **plus** included services and, for blocked/packaged offers, the flight (`composicionPrecio.ts`
components `hotel / vuelo / servicio / redondeo`). The hotel component per person is exactly `marcar(neto hotel, pctMk)` (`componentes()`), so the accommodation benefit **is isolable** without touching the bundle:
it is `marcar(Σ child net of the nights the promotion won, pctMk)` per free child, shown as its own signed line (same pattern as `cargoMascota` / `ajusteRedondeo`). Meal plan is part of the hotel net row (category × regimen),
so it travels with the accommodation. The bundle PVP of a child is never zeroed. Existing rounding stays in the `redondeo` component of the child line; the benefit is a separate line, so no passenger rows are added for rounding.

**Which child is free (corrected after the Codex audit, F2).** Every child now has a verifiable age ↔ room ↔ charged-tariff relationship (`asignarMenores`, `lib/hoteles/ninoGratis.ts`): children, in the order they were declared, occupy the rooms in order and, inside a room, the first is Niño 1 and the second Niño 2 (the convention already stated in `distribuirPorHabitaciones`). The free child is the FIRST ELIGIBLE child of the room in that order and what is waived is that child's REAL tariff (never «Niño 1 by default»): ages [14, 8] with a 3–12 range waive the Niño 2 tariff. The assignment is explained to the user in the signed line («hab. 1: niño de 8 años (Niño 2)») and in the quote conditions, including rooms where the benefit does not apply and why. If the promotion has its own age range and the exact ages are not declared, the benefit cannot be resolved and the quote is **rejected with an actionable message** (it is neither granted nor withheld silently).
**Limitation:** with a promotion-specific age range and exact ages that cannot be tied to rooms (mixed eligible/ineligible ages in several rooms), the benefit is granted only where it is safe under every possible assignment; otherwise it is withheld with a stated reason — no allocation is invented.
A stay combining «N noches, 1 gratis» with a free child uses the child's per-night net before that stay-level adjustment (documented edge, not reachable with the current promotion forms in a single promotion).

## 5. Pricing wiring (what each consumer does now)

The benefit is the **hotel net of the nights the promotion won, with the package markup, as its own signed line**. It never touches a PVP, a flight, assistance, transfers, tours or services.

| Consumer | File | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Hotel by dates | `lib/reservar/liquidacionHotel.ts` | `ComboCotizado.ninoGratisNoches` (Niño 1 / Niño 2 winning nights, nets adjusted like `costoHotel`: EBB/Long Stay discount, then optional cent rounding); omitted when empty; deleted with the other `reglasCierre` keys |
| Search by rooms + children | `lib/reservar/cotizar.ts` | `buscarHoteles` applies `calcularBeneficioNinoGratis` with the real per-room distribution (adults = `pax_tarifa`) and the Niño ages; subtracts from `total`; exposes `beneficioNinoGratis {total, descripcion}`; the public `cotizarPorFechas` is a whitelist, so no nets leak |
| Reservation / quotation | `lib/reservar/computo.ts` | Both branches collect the winning nights; benefit computed once, subtracted from `precioVenta`, exposed as `beneficioNinoGratis {total, descripcion, costoNeto}`; composition gets a `nino_gratis` adicional (Σ lines + adicionales = total) |
| Cost | `costoHotelPersona` (+ the contract site) | subtracts `costoNeto`, so `costo_hotel` / the hotel CxP reflect the free child |
| Contract / quotation items | `armarItems63`, both `reservar/actions.ts` item sites, cart conversion, `tarifario/checkout/actions.ts` | one signed item «Niño gratis por habitación · promo» (orden 203, `adultos:1 × tarifa_adulto = negative total`, like `ajusteRedondeo`; a zero-adult line would be worth 0 under `valorVisibleContratoItem`); description + amount only, no net / per-night detail / markup |
| Quotation breakdown | `components/cotizacion/ComposicionPrecio.tsx` | one extra label |

**Documented limitations after the corrections:** (1) the child↔room relationship follows the declared-order convention above; the quote forms have no per-room child entry, so which room a child sleeps in is that convention (made visible in the description); (2) a child under 2 years on a Niño tariff is rejected with an explanation (not computed); (3) the existing `distribuirPorHabitaciones` fills room 1 first, so two children in two rooms land in one room unless the hotel sets `chd_min` — existing behavior, not #125; (4) legacy manual counts (`ninos/ninos2/infantes` without `edadesMenores`) with minors declared are **rejected** when a free-child promotion won nights (declare each child's age), instead of quoting without the benefit; (5) TRM offers are now computed (sale converted with the offer TRM, cost kept in hotel currency) or rejected when the TRM is missing — see §12 for what was verified in a real database; (6) EBB / Long Stay is applied to the SUM of the payable nights (same function as the class net), so the waiver equals the child's payable accommodation exactly; (7) `BuscadorBooking` shows the combo total (benefit included) — see §12 for the search ↔ quote check.
