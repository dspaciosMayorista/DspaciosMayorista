## 2. Which documents render LIVE from the ficha vs from a snapshot (consequence of a staff correction)

Read from code on this branch; staff corrections update `aliados` only and never rewrite `ventas`, `aliados_b2b`, `cotizaciones`, `contrato_*` or `usuarios`.

- **Frozen text (unaffected by a correction):** contract documents (`ventas.agencia_nombre/freelance_nombre`, copied at contract creation); the display name on the cuenta de cobro header (`aliados_b2b.aliado` / `ventas.*_nombre`); the frozen `b2bAsistido` plan of an assisted quotation (`aliado_nombre`, `aliado_documento`, `aliado_tipo_documento`); the NETO seal (`cotizacion_neto`); `contrato_resumen_precios`.
- **Live from the ficha (changes with a correction):** the cuenta de cobro / estado de cuenta «DE (quien cobra)» block — document type+number, address, phone, e-mail, bank data (`resolverComisionB2B` → `aliadoInfo`). Until worker A's frozen snapshot is merged, **a re-print of an already issued cuenta de cobro shows the corrected document/phone/e-mail**; after the snapshot lands, an accepted version keeps its own copy. The portal profile and the internal Aliados list are live by definition.
- **Guard that blocks instead of drifting:** converting a frozen NETO quotation compares the live ficha (name, document, type) with the frozen plan (`compararIdentidadAliadoConCongelada`, `asistidaB2B.ts:416`). After a correction of name/document the old NETO quotation can no longer be converted (message `MENSAJE_IDENTIDAD_ALIADO_CAMBIO`) and must be re-quoted. This is the intended protection of the historical quotation, not a defect; the correction form states it before saving.
